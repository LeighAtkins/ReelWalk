## How the sources were found and fetched

- **Names and links.** Photographer names, page slugs and file links come from the `__NEXT_DATA__` of pexels.com search pages, read in the agent Chrome (port 9333). Each name is therefore confirmed against its clip id.
- **Files.** They were downloaded from `videos.pexels.com`: the 720×1280 rendition for portrait clips, and 1920×1080 or 2048×1080 for landscape ones (cropped to 9:16 by the reel's cover fit).
- **Licence.** Every clip is under the Pexels License, free to use.
- **Music.** Tracks were captured from their Pixabay pages, also in the agent Chrome, and are under the Pixabay Content License, free to use. They were converted to AAC for the local render only.

## Why these songs

The owner's rule is no novelty tracks; it should sound like a real Spotify-playlist song. Both are moody R&B instrumentals in the reference reel 5's family (a slow pop/R&B ballad).

- **"R&B Instrumental" (Sub_Clair).** Chosen for "a day at" because its structure carries the recipe. The beat is 80 BPM (750 ms, close to reel 5's 836 ms), and a real breakdown falls away at 72.9 s, then the full beat returns hard at 84.0 s. The reveal is placed on that return.
- **"Frank Ocean type beat" (Ruiz_).** Used for the walkthrough: 120 BPM with a steady groove and no break, which suits a music-led tour.

Neither was listened to by the agent that chose them; the choice rests on measured tempo, structure and genre tags. Rockot's "Romantic sad nostalgic beat" (73.75 BPM, closest to reel 5's tempo) was the third candidate. It was set aside because its only section change is a 17 s quiet intro.
