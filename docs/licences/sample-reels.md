# Landing page sample reels and drawn screens (ADR 0085): sources and licences

Made 2026-10-26 through the real product (workspace "ReelWalk", cmuy51nnw000h01adf9ks11xo): clips uploaded to the library, the default "Make it for me" cut (`/api/reels/:id/autopilot`, style `just-listed`, no facts, model cut) saved, then edited with MCP `apply_commands` (hook, end card, shot lengths, music) and exported on reelwalking.com.

All addresses and prices are fictional. Suburb names are real places; no listing exists. Footage is stock, not the properties named.

Photographer names come from Pexels' own search-result data (`__NEXT_DATA__` of pexels.com search pages, read in the agent Chrome on 2026-10-26), so each is confirmed against the clip id. Individual video pages sat behind Cloudflare's check and were not opened.

Before upload each clip was centre-cropped and scaled to 1080x1920, cut to at most 12 s, and its audio dropped.
