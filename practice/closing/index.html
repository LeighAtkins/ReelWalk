Closing words

最後に一言 · about 30 seconds

For 「最後に何かありますか」. Thank them, name one thing from the conversation that stuck with you (fill 【】 on the day), restate commitment, finish. Under 30 seconds.

Replace each 【】 with your own facts, and say the finished version aloud. The audio reads the placeholder text as written.

  1. 本日は貴重なお時間をいただき、ありがとうございました。

    Thank you very much for your valuable time today.

  2. お話を伺(うかが)って、【印象に残った点】がとても魅力的だと感じました。

    Hearing about 【what stood out】 made the role even more appealing to me.

  3. 日本語はまだ伸ばす余地がありますが、技術と姿勢で貢献(こうけん)したいです。

    My Japanese still has room to grow, but I want to contribute through my skills and attitude.

  4. 分からないことは素直に聞いて、早くチームの力になれるよう努めます。

    I'll ask openly when I don't understand, and work to become useful to the team quickly.

  5. ぜひ御社(おんしゃ)で働かせていただきたいと思っております。

    I would very much like to work with you.

  6. どうぞよろしくお願いいたします。

    Thank you.

To memorise: play it twice, shadow it once, then turn on Hide Japanese and say each line before tapping to check.
